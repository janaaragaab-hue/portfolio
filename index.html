About me

I'm Jana, a front-end developer based in New Cairo with a Data Science background. I build responsive, interactive websites with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, TypeScript and React, and I care as much about how an interface works as how it looks.

My Data Science studies shape how I build: I think about what people will see, what they will do, and what the data can tell us about both. I'm currently training in front-end development with the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative, working on unit testing, documentation and clean code habits alongside React, Node.js and Docker.

I enjoy turning an idea into something that runs in the browser, from a team-built car dealership website to a Python game that plans its own moves. I'm open to front-end and software engineering roles and to collaborations, so feel free to reach out.
